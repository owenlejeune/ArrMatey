ArrMatey is an all-in-one mobile companion for your home server media stack. Support for Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr, Bookshelf, Listenarr, Kapowarr, Prowlarr, Seerr, Bazarr, Tracearr, and Tautulli is included, alongside media server integrations (Plex, Jellyfin, and Emby), Wake-on-LAN management, and download clients (qBittorrent, SABnzbd, Deluge, Transmission, NZBGet, and rTorrent)!

<b>Combined Dashboard</b>
<ul>
<li>Centralized overview of all instances, download clients, requests, and continue watching</li>
<li>Customizable layout: add, remove, and reorder cards to fit your workflow</li>
<li>Continue Watching card for Plex, Jellyfin, and Emby media servers</li>
<li>Real-time network status, Wake-on-LAN controls, and detailed disk space monitoring</li>
<li>Quick-access shortcuts to your recently added media and upcoming schedule</li>
</ul>

<b>Multi-Instance & Media Server Support</b>
<ul>
<li>Manage multiple Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr, Bookshelf, Listenarr, and Kapowarr instances</li>
<li>Browse and sync Plex, Jellyfin, and Emby media server libraries with playback controls and watch status</li>
<li>Seamless integration with Prowlarr for indexers, Seerr for requests, Bazarr for subtitles, Chaptarr for audiobook chapters, and Tracearr/Tautulli for stream monitoring</li>
<li>Quick instance switching and custom instance labels</li>
</ul>

<b>Library Management</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse your entire media library with list and grid views</li>
<li>Search and filter by title, quality, and monitored status</li>
<li>Sort by name, date added, size, and more</li>
<li>View detailed media information, including episode lists, season management, and file status</li>
<li>Dedicated management for Audiobooks and Comics with metadata and file tracking</li>
</ul>

<b>Interactive Search</b>
<ul>
<li>Manual search for releases via Arr instances or Prowlarr</li>
<li>Filter by quality, language, indexer, and protocol</li>
<li>View seeders, size, age, and custom format scores</li>
<li>One-tap download with confirmation for rejected releases</li>
</ul>

<b>Calendar View</b>
<ul>
<li>View upcoming episodes, movies, albums, books, and audiobooks</li>
<li>Switch between list and month views with configurable first day of the week</li>
<li>Filter by content type, monitored status, and premieres/finales</li>
<li>Scroll-to-today for easy navigation</li>
</ul>

<b>Activity Queue & Blocklist</b>
<ul>
<li>Real-time download monitoring across all download clients (qBittorrent, SABnzbd, Deluge, Transmission, NZBGet, rTorrent)</li>
<li>View progress, ETA, and detailed status</li>
<li>Multi-select queue management with bulk removal options</li>
<li>Inspect and manage blocklisted releases across instances</li>
</ul>

<b>Advanced Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Android App Shortcuts:</b> Jump to services or search directly from your home screen</li>
<li><b>Wake-on-LAN:</b> Wake sleeping servers directly from within the app</li>
<li><b>Custom Headers:</b> Add custom HTTP headers per instance</li>
<li><b>Local Network Switching:</b> Automatically use local URLs when connected to home Wi-Fi</li>
<li><b>Slow Instance Mode:</b> Configurable timeout for remote or slower instances</li>
</ul>

<b>External Metadata</b>
<ul>
<li>Media artwork and metadata may be loaded directly from external services such as The Movie Database (TMDB) and TheTVDB.</li>
</ul>
