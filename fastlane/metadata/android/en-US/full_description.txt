ArrMatey is an all-in-one mobile companion for your home server media stack. Seamlessly manage your library, search for new content, and monitor your downloads from anywhere.

<b>Comprehensive App Support</b>
<li>
<ul><b>Sonarr:</b> TV series management and monitoring</ul>
<ul><b>Radarr:</b> Movie collection management</ul>
<ul><b>Lidarr:</b> Music library management</ul>
<ul><b>Readarr:</b> Ebook and audiobook collection manager</ul>
<ul><b>Listenarr:</b> Feature-rich audiobook management</ul>
<ul><b>Prowlarr:</b> Indexer management and proxying</ul>
<ul><b>Seerr:</b> Manage requests and issues via Overseas or Jellyseerr</ul>
</li>

<b>Multi-Instance Support</b>
<li>
<ul>Manage multiple instances per service</ul>
<ul>Quick instance switching and smart auto-hiding</ul>
<ul>Custom instance labels and configurations</ul>
<ul>Local network switching: Automatically use local URLs when on home Wi-Fi</ul>
</li>

<b>Library Management</b>
<li>
<ul>Browse your library with customizable list and grid views</ul>
<ul>Search and filter by title, quality, monitored status, and more</ul>
<ul>View detailed media information, including episode lists, file status, and metadata</ul>
<ul>Interactive season and album management</ul>
</li>

<b>Interactive & Automatic Search</b>
<li>
<ul>Manual search for releases across multiple indexers</ul>
<ul>Filter results by quality, language, size, and protocol</ul>
<ul>One-tap download with confirmation for rejected releases</ul>
<ul>Perform automatic searches for missing or wanted items</ul>

<b>Activity & Download Clients</b>
<li>
<ul>Real-time download monitoring for qBittorrent, SABnzbd, Deluge, and Transmission</ul>
<ul>View progress, ETA, speeds, and status</ul>
<ul>Cancel or blocklist downloads directly from the app</ul>
<ul>Manage your download queue with detailed item information</ul>
</li>

<b>Calendar & Upcoming Releases</b>
<li>
<ul>View upcoming episodes, movies, books, and album releases</ul>
<ul>Switch between list and monthly calendar views</ul>
<ul>Filter by content type, premieres, and finales</ul>
</li>

<b>Advanced Customization</b>
<li>
<ul><b>UI Personalization:</b> Adjust grid density, poster radius, and banner blur</ul>
<ul><b>Notifications:</b> Receive alerts for new releases and status updates</ul>
<ul><b>Custom Headers:</b> Add custom HTTP headers per instance</ul>
<ul><b>Slow Instance Mode:</b> Configurable timeouts for remote or slow connections</ul>
</li>
